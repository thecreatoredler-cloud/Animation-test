# Visual language: principles, not a fixed look

Benchmark: the broad strengths of the Zack D. Films Shorts (18 measured in the source project: still camera in a median 4 % of frames, about 0.9 % zoom per frame, about 3 cuts per 30 s, subject motion about 31 px/s). Our identity is **original**: no logos, characters or specific scenes copied.

## Always
1. **Immediate curiosity:** the subject is moving in frame 1 and the first caption is a question.
2. **Readability first:** one focal point per beat. DOF isolates it, but **never so shallow that the explained part goes soft** (v3 turn shot measured soft; v4 uses f/16 there).
3. **Context before macro:** establish the whole thing (≤ 0.7 s), then go close.
4. **Distinct beats:** each beat has its own scale or angle (context, contact, consequence, payoff).
5. **Believable materials:** roughness variation, bevelled edges, metal with *shaped* reflections (gradients), real-world scale.
6. **Cause and effect you can see:** dependent motion is driven by its cause.
7. **Continuous, motivated camera:** push in to explain, pull out to resolve, re-angle around rotations.
8. **Sound follows contact:** sounds come from visible touches. Silence carries the insight beat.

## Choose per subject (don't reuse the padlock's choices)
| Decision | Options | Choose by |
|---|---|---|
| Setting | real context (door, room, habitat, landscape) / stylised backdrop (anatomy) / metaphor space (concepts) | what makes the subject *exist* there |
| Palette | warm/cool contrast of subject vs environment | subject material: brass/sage (padlock); reds vs dark (vessel); blue-green (underwater); natural greens/ochres under a low sun (landscape) |
| Light | daylight + grazing fill + rim / motivated practical (window, endoscope lamp) / low sun for relief | what physically lights that place |
| Lens | 24–35 mm people and landscapes; 45–60 mm objects; 75–100 mm macro | shot purpose |
| Pace | 1 continuous move (mechanism), coverage cuts (characters), slow sweeps (landscapes) | archetype (`content-archetypes.md`) |

## Avoid
The padlock palette on everything · a static hero object in every opening · cutaways for topics that need a performance · the same push or orbit in every shot · generic grey studios · razor-shallow DOF · decorative particles, glow or whooshes · default-looking materials · mixed realism levels in one frame · copying a reference shot.

## Multi-style videos
Allowed and encouraged (a person → anatomy → metaphor → person). Keep a **visible style boundary** (a transition, a colour or grade shift) so a metaphor is never mistaken for literal anatomy or physics.
