## The three most important remaining weaknesses
1. **The sound is synthesised and unverified by ear (whole video, especially the key slide at 0.7–5.0 s).** Modal synthesis fixes masking and timing, but a real recording of a key entering a lock has a complexity my model only approximates. The next step: the owner listens. If it sounds artificial, replace `key_slide_start` and `shackle_pop` with licensed recordings (the recipe's `file` mode) and keep the event timing.
2. **Section continuity under direct sun (2.0–5.0 s).** The brushed section face catches the sun and reads paler than the shaded exterior brass next to it. It's better than v1's beige block but still not "obviously the same metal". Next: match the section's roughness and colour to the exterior under the same light, or tilt the sun so section and exterior get equal light.
3. **2.8 s of fairly frontal framing during the pin ride (2.2–5.0 s).** It's readable, but it's the least cinematic stretch. Next: a slow 10–15° orbit during the ride (keeping the pins readable), and a faster key at the start of the ride to give room.
