# Production plan — "The Ancient Computer Found Underwater" (part 1, 10.4 s)

**Status:** the owner asked directly for about 10 s of video from the supplied voice-over (2026-11-10), so this plan and the build were made in the same session. The plan is still open to correction.

## Source material
- **Voice-over:** supplied by the owner (ElevenLabs export, 65.5 s). All pauses were cut to about 70 ms: `vo-full-tight.mp3` (60.3 s, whole script). This part uses the first 10.4 s: `vo.mp3`.
- **Narration for this part** (transcribed by an offline recogniser; small words may be wrong, the owner should confirm):
  > "Why did the 2,000-year-old shipwreck have a computer on board? Spring, 1900. Greek sponge divers hide from the storm near a tiny island called Antikythera."
- **History used:** the Antikythera mechanism, a geared bronze astronomical calculator from a shipwreck of about 60 BC, found by sponge divers in 1900 off the Greek island of Antikythera.

## Environments, characters, props and assets
Everything is built in code in the toon style found in the reference study (flat painted colours, ink contour lines). No external models are used in this part.

- **Seabed:** rippled sand with moving caustics, wreck ribs and hull planks, fallen mast, about 16 amphorae, a marble bust, a bronze arm reaching out of the sand, rocks, swaying seaweed, a fish school, bubbles, light shafts, drifting particles.
- **The mechanism (hero prop):** shoebox-sized corroded bronze block in a broken wooden case, with one large four-spoke gear, three smaller gears and a dial ring.
- **Sea:** animated toon water with foam, painted gradient sky, graphic clouds, gulls; storm version with dark clouds, rain and lightning.
- **Sponge boat:** wooden Greek kaiki, white hull with blue stripe, mast and sail, hand air pump with wheel, copper diving helmet, hose coil, basket of sponges, crate marked "1900", small flag.
- **Island:** tiny rocky island with a cove, white houses with blue doors and lit windows, chapel with blue dome, windmill, olive trees, stone jetty, signpost.
- **Characters (original designs):**
  - **Captain Stavros:** navy fisherman's cap, big moustache, blue striped shirt. Signature colour: navy.
  - **Nikos the diver:** cream canvas diving suit, red neckerchief, curly hair. Signature colour: red.
  - **A small orange fish** with a face.
  - Our character style: off-white head with a thick ink line, small round nose, rosy cheeks, ears; bean body; ink-line limbs.
- **Font:** Fredoka Bold (SIL Open Font License), `assets/fonts/fredoka-700.woff2`, from the `@fontsource/fredoka` npm package.
- **Sound:** the owner's voice-over in front; synthesized underwater drone, bubbles, gear ticks, whoosh, gulls, wind, rain and thunder under it.

## Visual direction
- **Seabed:** deep blue-teal ambient, pale cyan light from above with shafts, warm gold glow from the mechanism (warm against cool).
- **Calm sea:** saturated blue sky, warm sun, white clouds, turquoise water.
- **Storm:** slate-blue sky, grey-green water with foam, cold light, white lightning flash; warm orange windows on the island.
- **Target colour values:** as in DESIGN_RULES.md section 3.

## Shot list
| # | Shot type | Camera move | Reveals / emphasizes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High wide, tilted | Fast dive and push toward the wreck, easing out | The wreck and the age of the place |
| 2 | Extreme close-up, shallow focus | Slow push with a small orbit | The "computer": gears glowing |
| 3 | Low hero angle from the waterline | Rise and drift with the boat | The boat, the year |
| 4 | Two-shot on deck | Slow push; snap closer and tilt on the lightning | The divers and the change of weather |
| 5 | Aerial | Crane up and back | How small the island is; the boat running for the cove |
| 6 | Foreground sign, boat behind | Slow push past the sign | The name |

Set-ups are joined by 6-frame cross-dissolves while both shots move. Shot 2 → 3 is covered by a burst of bubbles.

## Animation beats
- **Shot 1:** fish school crosses; seaweed sways; bubbles rise.
- **Shot 2:** gears start dark, then light up and turn on "computer" (2.49 s); fish leans in, then snaps to a shocked pose.
- **Shot 3:** boat rides the swell; sail and flag move; gulls circle.
- **Shot 4:** captain proud, diver holds up a sponge (5.33 s) → both look up, worried, as the sky darkens (6.69 s, "hide") → lightning (7.25 s, "storm"): captain throws his arms up, diver ducks under his sponge; boat rocks harder; rain starts.
- **Shot 5:** boat pitches through waves toward the cove; windmill spins; lightning flickers far off.
- **Shot 6:** sign swings in the wind; rain; boat rocks at the jetty; windows glow.
- **Characters** move pose-to-pose with holds, as in the references.

## Captions
One or two words, hard swap, no pop, at 70% of frame height, timed from the voice-over.
