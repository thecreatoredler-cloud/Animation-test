# Showcase 01 — "The Watchmaker" (10 s visual quality test)

Approved by the owner (watchmaker workshop, Poly Haven models allowed).

## Story
At night, in her cramped workshop, Augusta Penn seats the last tiny gear in a pocket watch, holds it to her ear… and every clock in the shop comes back to life.

## Character (original)
**Augusta Penn**, elderly watchmaker.
- **Head and face:** round head, grey hair with a bun pierced by a pencil, round glasses with a brass loupe that flips down over her right eye.
- **Clothes:** teal cardigan with rolled sleeves and red sleeve garters, mustard bow tie, leather apron with tools in the pocket.
- **Face rig:** separate eyes, lids, pupils, brows, mouth shapes and the loupe.
- **Hands:** open, pinch (tweezers), grip (watch) and point.

## Light
- **Key:** warm desk-lamp spotlight, with shadow.
- **Rim/fill:** cool moonlight through the window, with shadow on high quality.
- **Low ambient.**
- **Practicals:** lamp bulb, oil-lamp flame, street lamp outside.
- **Atmosphere:** a lamp cone with dust motes, a moonbeam through the window.
- **Grade:** warm/cool; bloom only on bright light sources.

## Shots
| # | Time | Shot | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–3.0 s | Slow dolly-in from the dark doorway past blurred foreground pocket watches on strings | Establish the shop, the quiet stopped clocks and Augusta at work |
| 2 | 3.0–5.6 s | Close-up insert over the bench, shallow depth of field | The interaction: tweezers lift the gear and seat it, it clicks and the watch starts |
| 3 | 5.6–8.0 s | Low-angle medium shot | She lifts the watch to her ear. Concentration, then surprise as the pendulums behind her start one by one |
| 4 | 8.0–10.0 s | Rising orbit pull-back (high angle) | Delight. The whole shop ticking; warm lamp against blue night |

## Animation beats
- **0–3 s:**
  - concentration face: one eye squinting behind the loupe, tongue poking out;
  - small breathing, tweezers hovering.
- **3.0–4.6 s:**
  - anticipation: the hand rises before the move;
  - the tweezers carry the gear over (arc), then a slow, precise descent;
  - the gear seats at 4.55 s with a "click", a small hand recoil and a pop of surprise in her eyes.
- **5.6–8 s:**
  - tweezers set down, the hand switches to grip;
  - dip-then-lift of the watch to her ear (IK); listening pose (head tilt, eyes up);
  - pendulums start at 6.9 and 7.2 s; surprise at 7.5 s; the loupe flips up.
- **8–10 s:**
  - delight (grin, shoulders up, bun bounces);
  - the clocks tick and the grandfather clock chimes; the lamp sways slightly.

## Sound
Watch ticking that builds into a rhythm, a gear click, wall-clock ticks, a grandfather-clock chime, a warm music sting and a low room tone. Synthesised, as in Test 02.
